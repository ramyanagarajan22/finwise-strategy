# The Solution · FinWise

> Module 2 · Acquisition & Activation. The onboarding solution and the Aha moment that makes value land.

## The problem we saw

FinWise is losing customers faster than it gains them. In every one of the last 13 months, more customers left than joined: 317 left and only 128 joined. It's like a bucket with a hole in it.

People do use FinWise, and they're using it more over time. But using it more doesn't make them stay or pay. Only 2 out of every 100 trial users become paying customers, and that number never moves.

So the problem isn't getting people in. It's that FinWise never becomes something they can't live without.

## Aha moment

The Aha moment is when a customer sees their own business clearly, month by month (how much cash they have, whether they made a profit, where their money went), and shares it with their accountant.

**Why this moment:** Seeing your own numbers is the value. Sharing them with your accountant turns FinWise from a tool you check alone into something you and your accountant rely on together every month. That's much harder to walk away from.

## Onboarding prototype

**Clickable prototype:** https://claude.ai/artifact/Acq2Zvzb9EEfSRx6iyjLnP

The prototype is for existing customers, because keeping them is FinWise's biggest problem. It has just 3 screens:

1. **Welcome back:** "What do you want to check today?" Cash flow, Profit or Expenses. One tap and they're there.
2. **Their dashboard:** one big, clear number at the top, a simple month-by-month chart, and three short tips in plain language. Tabs let them switch between Cash flow, Profit and Expenses.
3. **Invite your accountant:** one email field, so their accountant can see the same numbers every month.

## Why this activates

**How it addresses the retention problem:**

- **Customers get value right away.** No setup screens, no loading screens, no tour. One tap takes them to their own numbers. The faster they see value, the less likely they are to give up and leave.
- **Each visit answers one clear question.** "How much cash do I have?" "Did I make money?" "Where did my money go?" Clear answers give customers a reason to come back every month.
- **The accountant invite builds a habit.** Once their accountant is looking at FinWise too, it becomes part of how the business runs. Customers are much less likely to quit a tool their accountant also uses.

**What I removed:** I cut the flow from 5 screens to 3 by removing the steps that slowed people down: connecting data again, the loading screen and extra Continue buttons. Every extra step before value is a chance for someone to drop off.

**How I'd check it's working:** Compare customers who invited their accountant with those who didn't. If invited customers stay longer and leave less often, the idea works.#
