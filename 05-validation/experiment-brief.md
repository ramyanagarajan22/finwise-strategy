# The Validation · FinWise

> Module 5 · Experimentation Methods. The experiment brief that tests the bet.

## Method

**Method:** A/B Test

**Why:** We’re testing one change (the new 3-screen experience) against the current experience for existing customers. Retention takes weeks to show up, so a bandit can’t optimize in real time. A clean 50/50 A/B test gives a reliable answer on whether the new flow keeps customers longer.

_____

## Hypothesis + primary metric

If we give existing FinWise customers a one-tap “What do you want to check today?” view of their own numbers, plus an easy way to invite their accountant, we expect more customers to reach the Aha moment, which will reduce churn.

**Primary metric:** % of customers who reach the Aha moment (view their dashboard and invite their accountant) within 30 days, target: 20% relative increase in Aha-moment rate versus control

_Experiment: Retention test, Stop more customers from leaving than joining (317 left vs. 128 joined over 13 months) by making FinWise part of how the business runs every month, shared with their accountant._

_Testing A 3-screen flow: Welcome back with one-tap choice (Cash flow, Profit, Expenses), a simple dashboard with one big number, chart and three tips, and an accountant invite. on Existing paying customers, especially those in their first 6 months, when churn risk is highest. Current experience: Returning customers go through 5 screens (reconnecting data, a loading screen, extra Continue buttons) before seeing their numbers, and there’s no way to bring their accountant in.._

_Predicted outcome: Aha-moment rate increases 20% or more in the test group, and those customers return more often each month, an early sign of lower churn.. If successful: Roll out to 95% of customers and keep a 5% holdout for 6 months to confirm long-term churn drops. Then test ways to increase accountant invites, such as a reminder after the first visit.. If unsuccessful: Check where customers dropped off. If they viewed the dashboard but didn’t invite their accountant, test the invite’s wording or timing. If they didn’t return at all, test a monthly email with their key number.._

_____

## Guardrail + read date

- **Guardrail:** Monthly churn must not increase, and support tickets per customer must not rise by more than 5%
- **Read date:** 30 days after launch, no earlier. Keep tracking 90-day retention to confirm the effect lasts.
