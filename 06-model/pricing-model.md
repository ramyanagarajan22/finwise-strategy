# The Model · FinWise

> Module 6 · Pricing & Monetization. The pricing & packaging recommendation memo for FinWise.

## Stage + model

**Stage:** Stage 1 · Value Creation, Customers don’t keep finding FinWise useful. Only 40% of paying customers stay a full year, and every month more customers leave than join. Also, only 2 out of 100 trial users ever pay. FinWise needs to make the product stick before changing the price.

**Model:** Freemium, People see the value of finance software at the end of each month. A short free trial ends before they get there, so they’re asked to pay too early. A free plan gives them time to build a monthly habit. The downside is less money at first. Keeping the paid-only trial would bring in more money per customer, but conversion would stay stuck at 2%.

_____

## Recommendation

Replace the free trial with a free plan for new small-business customers. The free plan includes the cash-flow view and inviting your accountant. The paid plan adds profit and expense views, more history, and more people. Accountants never pay. Since each accountant works with many businesses, they can bring new customers to FinWise for free.

**The pricing bet:** Packaging matched to value. We’re testing whether asking customers to pay after they’ve shared their numbers, not at a fixed trial end date, gets more of them to pay and stay.

_____

## Model signal

This targets “accountant invited in the first month.” Customers reach the aha moment (seeing their numbers and sharing them with their accountant) before they’re asked to pay. Then they pay when they want more.
